# Story 6.1: Booking Management

## User Story
As a member, I want to view and manage my bookings so that I can track my reservations and make changes.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] My Bookings section with upcoming and past bookings
- [ ] Booking status: Confirmed, In Progress, Completed, Cancelled
- [ ] Modification options: Cancel
- [ ] No show policy enforcement - flat fee
- [ ] 24-hour advance notice requirement for cancellation
- [ ] Booking history
- [ ] Email for each booking with a confirmation number
- [ ] Support contact for booking issues - Admin
