# Story 7.3: Partner Analytics

## User Story
As a lounge partner, I want to view booking analytics so that I can understand usage patterns and optimize operations.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Weekly/monthly booking volume reports
- [ ] Revenue attribution from Luxe Pass bookings
- [ ] Repeat guest identification
- [ ] Downloadable reports in CSV
