# Story 9.1: Membership

## User Story
As a member, I want to understand my membership benefits so that I can maximize my Luxe Pass experience.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Clear membership display in profile (active/inactive)
- [ ] Benefits explanation
- [ ] Upgrade options to monthly subscribers and pricing (monthly → yearly price)
- [ ] Renewal date and auto-renewal settings
