1. What you both get

For every friend who joins LuxePass with your code, one complimentary day pass is credited to your account and one to theirs. A day pass admits one person to any lounge in the network for a single visit, and is worth $75.00 at the member rate.

2. What counts as a referral

Your friend must be new to LuxePass, must enter your code when they sign up, and must do so within 60 days of the invitation being sent. After 60 days the invitation expires and can be sent again. Only the first code entered on an account counts.

3. When the reward is credited

The pass shows as Pending from the moment your friend signs up, and is credited within 48 hours of their first membership payment settling. If that payment fails or is refunded, the pending reward is withdrawn and you are told why.

4. Using your day passes

Referral passes are additional to the 8 visits included with your membership, and are used before those included visits. They can be booked at any of the twelve lounges, subject to the same availability and cancellation rules as any other visit. They have no cash value, cannot be transferred or sold, and remain valid for as long as your membership is active.

5. Limits

Up to 10 referral day passes can be earned per membership year. One reward is paid per person referred, however many invitations they receive. You cannot refer yourself, and accounts that share a payment method or a home address count as one person.

6. Fair use

En Soleil may withdraw a reward, retire a code or close the programme where invitations are sent in bulk, where accounts are created to collect rewards, or where the terms are otherwise worked around. We write to you before doing so, and support can review the decision.

7. Changes to this programme

These terms may change with 30 days' notice by email. Passes already credited are unaffected by a change, and a pending reward is settled under the terms in force on the day the invitation was accepted.
